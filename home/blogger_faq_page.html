FAQ

The questions we get asked the most. Missing one? Ask us on Discord.

Is it really free?

Yes. The full rules are free to download, now and forever. No paywall, no premium edition, no “advanced rules” sold separately. It will never be monetized.

Why? What do you get out of it?

We made it because it’s the game we wanted to play ourselves. We decided to share it online, and that brought tons of great feedback, which made the game better and better – and it’s still improving.

…And the kick of having people enjoy something we made. We want more cool miniatures on the table: let your imagination run wild, kitbash cool stuff and rest assured that it can be fielded on the tabletop.

What models can I use?

Any. Fantasy, sci-fi, historical, official ranges, third-party, 3D prints, kitbashes, that half-painted thing from 2009. If you can build it, you can field it. Bitzkrieg 28 is designed for roughly 28–32mm miniatures, but nobody’s going to measure your models.

Do I need models from a specific company?

No. That’s the whole point. Bitzkrieg 28 doesn’t sell miniatures and doesn’t care where yours came from.

How do I make rules for my fighters?

With the Fighter Forge. It’s built right into the website and is the beating heart of the Bitzkrieg 28 engine. It’s pretty intuitive, but if you want a guide, the last section of the Core Rules walks you through it step by step.

How big is a game?

Each player fields 5–15 fighters, and a game takes about 90 minutes. That’s small enough to learn fast and play on a weeknight, and big enough that tactics matter.

What do I need to play?

  • Your fighters
  • Printed Fighter Cards for your fighters
  • A handful of D12s (no more than 6 per player)
  • A tape measure or measuring stick in inches
  • Some terrain (the more, the better)
  • A 30" × 22" playing area – smaller or bigger is no problem

Why a D12?

Because all the D12s are really sad that everyone prefers D6s. ;)

No, really: we’ve always been bothered by the lack of granularity in D6-based wargames. Does improving from 6+ to 5+ really compare to going from hitting 1/6 of the time to 1/3? This was our chance to change that, so we did.

Is it fantasy or sci-fi?

Both, and neither. The engine is genre-neutral. It powers Mortalis, a grimdark future skirmish game, and Wrath & Ruin, a dark fantasy skirmish game, but you can use it with anything in your collection.

Everything in the Fighter Forge can be renamed, so a gun called “Soulreaper Cannon” in the grimdark future can become a “Tsunami Arms Helix” in your cyberpunk game. The rules stay the same and the immersion is kept.

Is close combat worth it?

Yes. Melee is a real tactic in Bitzkrieg 28, not a desperate last resort. Charging in with a rusty axe is a legitimate plan. Gun lines are boring – hit stuff up close!

What’s a “living rulebook”?

The rules are never finished. We update them regularly based on playtesting and suggestions from the community. Each update is versioned and dated, so you always know you’ve got the latest rules. See every change in the changelog.

How can I suggest a rule or report a problem?

Join our Discord and post it there. Every suggestion is read, and good ideas make it into the next update. You don’t have to be a game designer; “this felt clunky” is valuable feedback too.

Can I play solo or with more than two players?

Yes, although the solo rules are a little barebones at the moment. Expect them to be fleshed out as we go along.

Can I use it in tournaments or at my club?

Absolutely – and we’d love it if you shared pictures or stories on our Discord.

Can I share or print the rules?

Yes. Print them, share them, bring them to your club. Just don’t sell them.

Why is it called Bitzkrieg 28?

Bitz is hobby slang for spare model parts, the stuff in every hobbyist’s bitz box. Krieg is German for war. The 28 is a nod to the awesome Inq28 movement of the 2010s, where people built amazing models without caring what rules they had – the models always came first.

Put them together and you get a game about raiding your bitz box, making stuff you like and going to war with it.
