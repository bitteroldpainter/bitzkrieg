Bitzkrieg 28 Tokens

A set of custom tokens for 3D printing, as a .zip file. We recommend printing them at 3 mm height, but any size works.

Tip: for easy reading, fill the indents with paint. When it's dry, a quick pass with 400–600 grit sandpaper leaves the tops smooth and free of stray paint.

Mortalis Dice Tracker

A custom dice tracker for Feat Points, Victory Points and the turn number, as a .zip file.

Tip: print at the highest resolution your FDM printer allows; ours is printed at 0.06 mm layer height with a 0.2 mm nozzle. Superglue the top to the bottom and it's ready for priming and paint.
