A free skirmish engine for 28mm models

Every model deserves a fight.

Bitzkrieg 28 is a fast, gritty skirmish game for 5–15 fighters a side. You build your fighters to match the models you already own, so every kitbash, conversion and bits-box hero gets rules of its own.

Play it in the grimdark future with Mortalis, in a dark fantasy world with Wrath & Ruin, or in any setting you dream up.

Why Bitzkrieg 28?

5–15 fighters

Small enough to paint in a month, big enough to tell a story. Every fighter matters.

Games in about 90 minutes

Quick turns, no bookkeeping marathons. Two games in an evening is normal.

The D12 system

Twelve sides give finer odds than a D6, so a veteran really does feel better than a recruit.

Build what you own

The Fighter Forge prices any combination of weapons and abilities. WYSIWYG, always.

Close combat matters

Charges, counter-attacks and brutal melee weapons. Guns aren't the only answer.

Free. Forever.

Rules, builders and calculators cost nothing. No paywall, no models to buy from us.

Pick your battlefield

One rules engine, two ready-made worlds. Each has its own weapons, abilities and powers, and the same Fighter Forge builds both.

Grimdark future

Hive gangs, penal troopers and xenos raiders fight over the ruins of a dying galaxy. Guns, grenades and Psykers.

From bits box to battlefield

  1. [PHOTO: a kitbash in progress]

    1. Kitbash

    Build and paint the models as you want them to be – no restrictions. Any range, any scale close to 28mm, any mix of parts.

  2. [SCREENSHOT: a fighter card in the Fighter Forge]

    2. Forge

    Pick the weapons and abilities your model actually has. The Forge works out the points and prints the card.

  3. [PHOTO: two warbands mid-game]

    3. Fight

    Grab a friend and some terrain. Watch your creations come to life, beat each other to a pulp, and walk away with stories worth retelling.
