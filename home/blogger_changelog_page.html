Balance pass

Points & stats

  • A 3rd AP now costs 7 pts, and AP is capped at 3. Fighters with 4 AP are no longer possible.
  • Fast and Agent now cost 3 pts.

Abilities

  • Sustained Fire and Aggressive replace Hyper Aggressive. Sustained Fire allows two Shoot actions with Pistols, Light or Medium weapons, costs the same as the Fighter's most expensive ranged weapon, and is limited to 1 per 3 Fighters. Aggressive (2 pts) allows two Fight actions.
  • Gunslinger reworked. The second Shoot action made with a pistol in an activation may be free; it costs the same as the Fighter's most expensive pistol.
  • Patient and Reactive now gives one free action per turn. It costs 17 pts and cannot be combined with Sustained Fire.

Weapons

  • The Boost (1) weapon upgrade has been removed. It was too powerful for its cost.
