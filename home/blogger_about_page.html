Meet the Bitzkriegers.

The Bitzkrieg 28 Crew are a motley assembly of hobbyists hailing from the Cold Northern Wastes (also referred to as Denmark). They were drawn together like moths to a flame, amazed at Rasmus’ incredible project. Or pitied his useless attempts at making something actually playable, and decided to help him out. Your call.

Whatever the reason they’ve become Bitzkriegers, they are all invaluable parts of the team behind the game system, and have helped it become what it is.

Bitter Old Painter/GuitaRasmus/Rasmus Rask:Originator and initial creator of the Bitzkrieg 28 system. He is also the “graphics guy” who does the layout and graphics design on the websites, rulebooks, apps, etc, and the one to moan to when the app doesn’t work as intended. Loves cutting up miniatures and putting them together in ways they were never supposed to, and whines (a lot) when he actually has to paint them.

Martin Lottrup:Ork enthusiast extraordinaire and all round wargame enthusiast, with a slightly odd affinity for knowing dice statistics without being able to explain why. Has an ork top knot hairdo and has never explained why.

Kenneth Ryberg Andersen:Has spent most of his life pushing plastic miniatures to innocent youngsters in various gaming stores, and has an encyclopedic knowledge of 40k lore. He LOVES Space Wolves. Almost worryingly so.

Andreas Kentorp:Has played (and owns) more wargames and boardgames than he dares to admit to his girlfriend, and has an impressive grasp of the math behind game mechanics. Paints to a standard most people only dream of, and loves fantasy settings.

Thomas Jakobsen:If you can think of an RPG, Boardgame or Wargame from the past 40 years, chances are that Thomas has played it and remembers the rules. Grand Old Man of our crew, and a great idea generator. Still bitter about a game of Warhammer Fantasy 30 something years ago, where Rasmus made his whole Skaven army run away on turn 1.

Alexander M. Jensen:Our resident lab rat (he actually works in a lab, mind you). He has an impressive ability to grasp concepts and rules and to “look through the Matrix”, like Neo in the films - maybe that’s what the “M” in his name stands for?

Andreas Drægert:Very enthusiastic wargamer, but has the worst luck with dice. He’s recently used 6 Feat points for rerolls for a 5+ save, failing every one. Loves fantasy and is trying to learn every one of Tolkien’s LoTR languages… Or at least he references words nobody else understands.

Mathias K. Bay:Plays Tau, but we still like him. He’s an engineer, so he’s also the only one in our team that has proof that he’s not a complete nincompoop. He’s mostly into 40k and loves all kinds of boardgames, and also dabbles with 3d sculpting.

Aside from the crew, a whole bunch of people have also helped out with great suggestions, playtesting, listening to our endless rants, and the latter especially makes them deserve an honourable mention:

Rune Von Schlosser, Thomas Tønnesen, Allan Jørgensen, Mathias Bech, Yen The, Liver Ripamonti and Dominik Hupps.
